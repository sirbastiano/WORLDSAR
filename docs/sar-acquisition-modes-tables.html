README

SAR acquisition modes: resolution and polarization overview (public specs)
Interpretation
• Sentinel-1: spatial resolution values are single-look and typically given as range × azimuth (for SLC/GRD characteristics).
• NISAR: spatial resolution values are stated as ~7 m along-track (azimuth) and a slant-range range that depends on bandwidth/mode; table is from NASA Earthdata NISAR platform page.
• BIOMASS: SLC (single-look complex) resolution is from ESA BIOMASS seminar deck; an alternative published number (8.3 m azimuth, 59 m in-ground range) is from a peer-reviewed BIOMASS processor paper.
• TerraSAR-X: resolution values correspond to Single Look Slant Range Complex (SSC) products as stated in the Airbus TerraSAR-X Image Product Guide; ground-range resolution depends on incidence angle.
• Always verify the exact product (SLC/GRD/SSC/GSLC etc.) and any multilooking/resampling used in your processing chain before comparing sensors.
